Licht & Peasants (2023)

Lich & Peasants is a strategy game created for the GMTK Game Jam 2023.

The player controls a lich and must prevent peasants from destroying its magical artifacts.

Language

C#

Unity

Game type

Strategy

Dev time

2 days

Game features

- The theme of the Game Jam was 'Roles reversed'. That's why you play as the villain. The page and the description of the game were also written to make people believe that they were going to play the role of the good peasants.

- The player must prevent the peasants from reaching the lich room. All rooms except those adjacent to the lich's are by default obscured.

- To succeed, the player must use the various runes of the lich to see, scare, or burn the peasants in a room. Some peasants are resistant to the flame spell and the scare spell.

- You wins when you have survived long enough, or when all the peasants have died.

- There is a campaign with some premade levels, as well as an endless mode where the manor of the lich is procedurally generated.
