Operative (2023)

Operative is an action/infiltration FPS coded in Unity.

The player must discreetly or not perform objectives in various levels. (akin to Payday)

Language

C#

Unity

Game type

FPS

Dev time

3 months

Game features

- The game is divided into playable levels and cutscenes.

- Each mission is doable discreetly or not. If you are spotted, the police will come in waves to kill you.

- Completing a mission gives money and XP, depending on whether you managed to do it discreetly or not.

- At each level of EXP earned, you can spend a skill point in one of the available skills.

- There are several types of NPCs: Civilians, who cannot defend themselves. Guards, who can defend themselves. And the police, who are better armed than the guards.

- The NPCs are patrolling and will call the police if they see anything suspicious.

- You can buy different weapons with the money earned from the mission to gain an advantage over NPCs.

- You can interact in different ways with the scenery: hide bodies in bins, break windows, steal clothes, ...
